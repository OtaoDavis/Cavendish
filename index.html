Cavendish Africa is an education company that provides university and business education programs that prepare learners for employment, entrepreneurship and leadership in Africa and around the world.

Our mission is to provide high-impact academic frameworks that prepare learners for employment, entrepreneurship, and leadership, equipping them with the mindset and skills to succeed in Africa and around the world.

Connecting Learning
to Opportunity.

Across Africa, millions of young people graduate into economies where formal employment opportunities remain limited. Preparing learners for success requires more than education—it requires connecting learning to real pathways for employment, entrepreneurship, and economic opportunity.

Through innovative programs, our institutions help learners access opportunities in Africa and the global digital economy while gaining practical experience supporting entrepreneurs growing businesses.
