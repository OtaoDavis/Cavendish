Entrepreneurs Drive Growth

Entrepreneurs and small businesses are among the most important drivers of employment, innovation, and economic growth across Africa. Their success creates opportunity, not only for themselves, but for employees, families and communities.

Building a Business is Challenging

Entrepreneurs face complex challenges every day — from strategy and operations to leading people, managing finances and growth. Without practical support, opportunities to learn, and a strong community, entrepreneurship can become a lonely journey.

Business Growth Requires Leadership

Successful businesses are built by leaders who can navigate challenges, make sound decisions, and inspire others to achieve a shared vision. Developing this leadership capacity is essential for long-term growth and impact.

That’s Why We built CABS

We believe entrepreneurs deserve a different kind of business education. We created CABS to help ambitious entrepreneurs become better leaders, build stronger business, and create greater impact. Everything we do is built on three guiding principles that shape how entrepreneurs learn and grow.

CABS is Designed for
Busy Entrepreneurs.

Start Here

Every entrepreneur begins with Entrepreneurial Leadership
(8 weeks), the foundation for everything that follows.

Learn at Your Pace

After Entrepreneurial Leadership, complete one or two parts at time (8 weeks). If your business needs more of your time, simply pause and continue with the next part when you are ready.

Build Over Time

The full CABS journey is designed to be completed over 1 year (2 parts at time) to 2 years (1 part at time), giving you time to apply each lesson, strengthen your leadership, and build a stronger business.

Learn From Anywhere

Live online and recorded sessions allow you to learn from anywhere, while practical assignments help you improve your business along the way.

Weekly Commitment

Plan for 4-6 hours per week, including live sessions, independent learning and applying lessons in your business
